SAGA Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

About SAGA

SAGA is an educational collaboration platform for managing Courses, Teams, and Projects. It can connect a Project to Jira Cloud and GitHub when an authorized user chooses to do so.

Information we process

We process account and profile information, such as name, email address, role, and account status; Course, Team, Project, and membership information; and integration information needed to operate an authorized Jira Cloud or GitHub connection. We may also process operational records such as invitation and integration activity.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, provide Course, Team, and Project features, manage authorized integrations, maintain service security, troubleshoot, and meet applicable educational or legal obligations.

OAuth and credentials

OAuth and provider credentials are handled on the backend and are not returned to the frontend. Credentials are protected by the implementation and used only to provide authorized integration functionality.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with the infrastructure and service providers needed to operate SAGA, and with Jira Cloud or GitHub when a user authorizes the relevant integration, subject to the applicable provider terms.

Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to operate SAGA, maintain Course and Project records, resolve issues, and satisfy applicable obligations. Retention may vary by institution, Course, and integration configuration.

Your choices

You may use the existing account and integration controls available to you to stop using an integration or disconnect it where that option is provided. For requests about your information, contact the SAGA operator.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children and educational use

SAGA is intended for educational collaboration. Institutions and users are responsible for ensuring that use of SAGA is appropriate for their educational setting and applicable requirements for children or students.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SAGA changes. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.
